# Code of Conduct

MetaCore AI welcomes technical discussion and contributions from people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

Participants are expected to:

- Communicate respectfully and focus criticism on code, behavior, and engineering tradeoffs.
- Provide enough technical context for others to understand and reproduce a problem.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, spam, and deliberate disruption.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's credentials or local data.
- Follow project security requirements when discussing filesystem access, AI providers, and hardware behavior.

Repository maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content that violates these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its contributors.

中文说明：参与者应保持尊重，围绕代码、行为和工程取舍进行讨论，不得发布他人的凭据、本地数据或其他隐私信息。维护者可以处理违反上述要求的内容，并在必要时限制相关账号参与项目。
